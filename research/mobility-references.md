# Mobility and Stability Form References

Scope: nine mobility/stability guides in `js/data/exercises.js`. These are research links, not copied media. “Exact” means the named Army drill; “component model” means a credible visual reference for one movement within a drill. Consult the current ATP before treating an educational analog as doctrine.

## Bend and Reach

**H2F cue cross-check:** ATP 7-22.02 Preparation Drill, exercise 1, is the controlling model: straddle stance, arms overhead, squat and reach through the legs, then return; keep the heels down. This agrees with the app cues.

1. [U.S. Army Publishing Directorate — ATP 7-22.02, Holistic Health and Fitness Drills and Exercises (Preparation Drill)](https://armypubs.army.mil/epubs/DR_pubs/DR_a/ARN42012-ATP_7-22.02-000-WEB-1.pdf): Exact Army sequence; use its start, bottom, and recovery illustrations as the primary pose set.
2. [U.S. Army Center for Initial Military Training — Physical Readiness Training](https://www.army.mil/article/240335/army_combat_fitness_test): Army PRT context; preserve a deliberate, coached drill cadence rather than a fitness-editorial squat.
3. [ACE — Bodyweight Squat](https://www.acefitness.org/resources/everyone/exercise-library/135/bodyweight-squat/): Component model for a full-foot squat: knees follow toes and torso remains organized.
4. [ExRx.net — Bodyweight Squat](https://exrx.net/WeightExercises/Quadriceps/BWSquat): Side-view component reference for hip/knee depth and planted heels.
5. [Hospital for Special Surgery — How to Do a Squat](https://www.hss.edu/article_squats.asp): Clinical teaching model for neutral trunk and controlled descent.


## Rear Lunge

**H2F cue cross-check:** ATP 7-22.02 identifies Rear Lunge as Preparation Drill exercise 2 and also includes it in the Recovery Drill. The active version steps back to the ball of the foot with square hips; the Recovery Drill version is a held stretch. Do not blend the two tempos in one model.

1. [U.S. Army Publishing Directorate — ATP 7-22.02 (Preparation and Recovery Drills)](https://armypubs.army.mil/epubs/DR_pubs/DR_a/ARN42012-ATP_7-22.02-000-WEB-1.pdf): Exact source for both Army uses; label the selected variant and cadence.
2. [ACE — Reverse Lunge](https://www.acefitness.org/resources/everyone/exercise-library/366/reverse-lunge/): Component model for stepping backward without driving the lead knee inward.
3. [ExRx.net — Bodyweight Reverse Lunge](https://exrx.net/WeightExercises/Quadriceps/BWLungeRear): Side and front checkpoints for a vertical trunk and controlled return.
4. [Hospital for Special Surgery — Lunges](https://www.hss.edu/conditions_lunges.asp): Clinical visual cue: lead knee tracks over the foot rather than collapsing medially.
5. [American Academy of Orthopaedic Surgeons — Hip Conditioning Program](https://orthoinfo.aaos.org/en/recovery/hip-conditioning-program/): Stretching/hip-position reference for the recovery interpretation.


## High Jumper

**H2F cue cross-check:** ATP 7-22.02 Preparation Drill exercise 3 controls the named movement. The app’s small jump, arm swing, and soft landing cues should read as a low-amplitude preparation movement, not a maximal vertical jump.

1. [U.S. Army Publishing Directorate — ATP 7-22.02 (Preparation Drill, High Jumper)](https://armypubs.army.mil/epubs/DR_pubs/DR_a/ARN42012-ATP_7-22.02-000-WEB-1.pdf): Exact drill reference; use the prescribed phase order and moderate cadence.
2. [ACE — Squat Jump](https://www.acefitness.org/resources/everyone/exercise-library/80/squat-jump/): Component reference for takeoff and a two-foot landing.
3. [ExRx.net — Vertical Jump](https://exrx.net/Testing/VerticalJump): Visual reference for coordinated arm swing and extension.
4. [Nationwide Children’s Hospital — Jumping and Landing](https://www.nationwidechildrens.org/specialties/sports-medicine/sports-medicine-articles/jumping-and-landing): Education reference for quiet, flexed-hip/knee landing mechanics.
5. [American Academy of Pediatrics — Plyometric Exercises](https://www.healthychildren.org/English/healthy-living/sports/Pages/Plyometric-Exercises.aspx): Youth-sports teaching reference; land under control before the next repetition.


## Windmill

**H2F cue cross-check:** ATP 7-22.02 Preparation Drill exercise 6 is the controlling named movement: use a straddle stance, rotate and bend toward the outside foot, and keep the opposite arm high. The model must preserve rotation rather than depicting a plain side bend.

1. [U.S. Army Publishing Directorate — ATP 7-22.02 (Preparation Drill, Windmill)](https://armypubs.army.mil/epubs/DR_pubs/DR_a/ARN42012-ATP_7-22.02-000-WEB-1.pdf): Exact Army positional reference.
2. [ACE — Standing Toe Touch](https://www.acefitness.org/resources/everyone/exercise-library/60/standing-toe-touch/): Component reference for a controlled standing reach, not forced end range.
3. [ExRx.net — Standing Trunk Rotation](https://exrx.net/Stretches/Obliques/StandingRotation): Component reference for torso rotation while the pelvis stays organized.
4. [Hospital for Special Surgery — Back Stretching Exercises](https://www.hss.edu/health-library/move-better/back-stretches): Clinical range-of-motion reference; move only within a pain-free range.
5. [American Council on Exercise — Flexibility Training](https://www.acefitness.org/resources/pros/expert-articles/5929/flexibility-training/): Teaching reference for controlled, non-ballistic range.


## Prone Row

**H2F cue cross-check:** ATP 7-22.02 Preparation Drill exercise 8 is the named form source. The app cues require prone setup, a modest head/chest lift, arms brought to shoulder level, and a hold; avoid turning it into a loaded gym row.

1. [U.S. Army Publishing Directorate — ATP 7-22.02 (Preparation Drill, Prone Row)](https://armypubs.army.mil/epubs/DR_pubs/DR_a/ARN42012-ATP_7-22.02-000-WEB-1.pdf): Exact Army form and hold position.
2. [ACE — Prone Cobra](https://www.acefitness.org/resources/everyone/exercise-library/283/prone-cobra/): Component model for prone posterior-shoulder activation and modest chest lift.
3. [ExRx.net — Prone Shoulder Extension](https://exrx.net/WeightExercises/DeltoidPosterior/DBProneShoulderExtension): Component reference for shoulder-blade retraction without neck cranking.
4. [Hospital for Special Surgery — Exercises for Better Posture](https://www.hss.edu/health-library/move-better/posture-exercises): Educational visual model for long neck and scapular control.
5. [American Academy of Orthopaedic Surgeons — Shoulder Conditioning Program](https://orthoinfo.aaos.org/en/recovery/shoulder-conditioning-program/): Clinical shoulder-blade and pain-free-range reference.


## Shoulder Stability Drill (I/Y/T/L/W)

**H2F cue cross-check:** The app correctly identifies five slow, prone letter patterns. ATP 7-22.02 is the doctrinal order and cadence check; component sources below help distinguish each letter silhouette. Keep this a bodyweight/control drill unless a source explicitly marks a loaded variation.

1. [U.S. Army Publishing Directorate — ATP 7-22.02 (Shoulder Stability Drill)](https://armypubs.army.mil/epubs/DR_pubs/DR_a/ARN42012-ATP_7-22.02-000-WEB-1.pdf): Exact Army drill, including I/Y/T/L/W sequence and slow cadence.
2. [American Academy of Orthopaedic Surgeons — Shoulder Conditioning Program](https://orthoinfo.aaos.org/en/recovery/shoulder-conditioning-program/): Clinician-reviewed visual reference for shoulder-blade control and safe range.
3. [Hospital for Special Surgery — Shoulder Exercises](https://www.hss.edu/health-library/move-better/shoulder-exercises): Education reference for low-load shoulder stabilization positions.
4. [ExRx.net — Prone Shoulder Horizontal Abduction](https://exrx.net/WeightExercises/DeltoidPosterior/DBProneShoulderHorizontalAbduction): “T” component model; arms stay at shoulder level.
5. [ExRx.net — Prone Shoulder Scaption](https://exrx.net/WeightExercises/DeltoidPosterior/DBProneShoulderScaption): “Y” component model; use the diagonal arm angle, not a shrug.


## Hip Stability Drill (HSD)

**H2F cue cross-check:** ATP 7-22.02 controls the HSD’s exercise sequence and five-repetition, slow-control character. The app data intentionally summarizes it broadly; use the exact ATP plate before selecting component movements for a production model.

1. [U.S. Army Publishing Directorate — ATP 7-22.02 (Hip Stability Drill)](https://armypubs.army.mil/epubs/DR_pubs/DR_a/ARN42012-ATP_7-22.02-000-WEB-1.pdf): Exact Army sequence and form illustrations.
2. [American Academy of Orthopaedic Surgeons — Hip Conditioning Program](https://orthoinfo.aaos.org/en/recovery/hip-conditioning-program/): Clinician-reviewed component drawings for hip control exercises.
3. [Hospital for Special Surgery — Hip Strengthening Exercises](https://www.hss.edu/health-library/move-better/hip-strengthening-exercises): Visual reference for controlled glute/hip positioning.
4. [ACE — Glute Bridge](https://www.acefitness.org/resources/everyone/exercise-library/273/glute-bridge/): Component model for a bridge-style hip extension without lumbar overextension.
5. [ExRx.net — Side Lying Hip Abduction](https://exrx.net/WeightExercises/HipAbductor/BWSideLyingHipAbduction): Component model for pelvis-stable lateral hip control.


## Recovery Drill (RD) Static Stretches

**H2F cue cross-check:** ATP 7-22.02 and the app agree that RD closes every PRT session and includes Overhead Arm Pull, Rear Lunge, Extend and Flex, Thigh Stretch, Single-Leg Over, Groin Stretch, Calf Stretch, and Hamstring Stretch. Present holds as calm, pain-free positions, up to 30 seconds; do not animate them as warm-up movements.

1. [U.S. Army Publishing Directorate — ATP 7-22.02 (Recovery Drill)](https://armypubs.army.mil/epubs/DR_pubs/DR_a/ARN42012-ATP_7-22.02-000-WEB-1.pdf): Exact eight-stretch sequence and Army form plates.
2. [American Academy of Orthopaedic Surgeons — Stretching Exercises](https://orthoinfo.aaos.org/en/recovery/stretching-exercises/): Clinician-reviewed static-stretch visual library and pain-free-range framing.
3. [Hospital for Special Surgery — Stretching Exercises](https://www.hss.edu/health-library/move-better/stretching-exercises): Visual reference for sustained, controlled holds and breathing.
4. [ACE — Static Stretching](https://www.acefitness.org/resources/everyone/blog/6593/what-is-static-stretching/): Teaching reference distinguishing a hold from bouncing.
5. [Mayo Clinic — Stretching: Focus on Flexibility](https://www.mayoclinic.org/healthy-lifestyle/fitness/in-depth/stretching/art-20047931): Patient-education reference for gradual, non-painful holds.


## PMCS Drill

**H2F cue cross-check:** ATP 7-22.02 defines PMCS as a pre-PRT or Recovery Drill enhancement used to check stiffness or pain around a joint. The app’s spine, ankle, knee, hip, shoulder, elbow, and wrist ranges are a screening sequence, not a flexibility workout; all depictions should remain pain-free and controlled.

1. [U.S. Army Publishing Directorate — ATP 7-22.02 (PMCS Drill)](https://armypubs.army.mil/epubs/DR_pubs/DR_a/ARN42012-ATP_7-22.02-000-WEB-1.pdf): Exact Army purpose, sequence, and form illustrations; primary reference.
2. [American Academy of Orthopaedic Surgeons — Conditioning Program](https://orthoinfo.aaos.org/en/recovery/conditioning-program/): Clinician-reviewed joint mobility component drawings.
3. [Hospital for Special Surgery — Mobility Exercises](https://www.hss.edu/health-library/move-better/mobility-exercises): Education reference for slow, comfortable range-of-motion checks.
4. [Mayo Clinic — Stretching: Focus on Flexibility](https://www.mayoclinic.org/healthy-lifestyle/fitness/in-depth/stretching/art-20047931): Safety model: do not force movement through pain.
5. [Centers for Disease Control and Prevention — Flexibility Exercise](https://www.cdc.gov/physical-activity-basics/about/index.html): Public-health framing for controlled movement and individual ability limits.


## Count
