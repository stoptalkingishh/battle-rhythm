# Anaerobic-Endurance Form Reference Index

Research date: 2026-08-14. Scope is limited to the seven `anaerobic-endurance` activities in `js/data/exercises.js`. Links are public references, not embedded or downloaded media. A takeaway describes the single most useful pose, path, or timing relationship to model; it is not a substitute for unit coaching, risk management, or the publication's full instructions.

## 30:60s

**H2F cue cross-check:** FM 7-22 classifies 30:60s as anaerobic endurance. The app's 30-second maximum-effort run, 60-second walk, warm-up, and arm-drive cues are compatible with that classification; the sources below support sprint posture and front-side mechanics, not a new Army prescription.

1. [HQDA, *FM 7-22: Holistic Health and Fitness*, table 6-2](https://armypubs.army.mil/epubs/DR_pubs/DR_a/ARN44522-FM_7-22-002-WEB-7.pdf). **Visual-model takeaway:** show a straight sprint lane divided into a 30-second work segment and a 60-second walking-recovery segment.
2. [U.S. Track & Field and Cross Country Coaches Association, *Sprint Mechanics*](https://www.ustfccca.org/ustfccca-info/sprint-mechanics). **Visual-model takeaway:** model tall hips, a neutral head, and the foot landing under the body rather than reaching in front.
3. [National Strength and Conditioning Association, *Sprint Mechanics*](https://www.nsca.com/education/articles/kinetic-select/sprint-mechanics/). **Visual-model takeaway:** use an upright acceleration/maximum-velocity silhouette with opposite arm and leg action.
4. [World Athletics, *Introduction to sprinting*](https://worldathletics.org/kids-athletics). **Visual-model takeaway:** make the arm action a shoulder-driven forward/back swing, with elbows bent, rather than a cross-body sweep.
5. [Human Kinetics, *How to improve sprinting technique*](https://us.humankinetics.com/blogs/excerpt/how-to-improve-sprinting-technique). **Visual-model takeaway:** depict a dorsiflexed swing foot returning down beneath the hips.

**Model baseline:**
- Original SVG requirement 1: a side-view runner with head, torso, pelvis, knee, and ankle landmarks; no copied photo or frame.
- Original SVG requirement 2: a two-state interval bar labeled `SPRINT 30 s` and `WALK 60 s`, using shape and text rather than source artwork.
- Original SVG requirement 3: three callouts only: tall posture, elbows drive back, foot under hips.

## 60:120s

**H2F cue cross-check:** FM 7-22 lists 60:120s under anaerobic endurance. The current hard-run, pace-holding, and 120-second walking-recovery cues fit the activity; compared with 30:60s, the visual should communicate repeatable hard running rather than an all-out finishing lean.

1. [HQDA, *FM 7-22: Holistic Health and Fitness*, table 6-2](https://armypubs.army.mil/epubs/DR_pubs/DR_a/ARN44522-FM_7-22-002-WEB-7.pdf). **Visual-model takeaway:** distinguish the 60-second work interval from the longer 120-second walk recovery with a 1:2 timeline.
2. [U.S. Army H2F, *Physical Domain*](https://h2f.army.mil/Domains/Physical-Domain/). **Visual-model takeaway:** represent interval running as a programmed physical-readiness task, with a measured lane and controlled repeat sequence.
3. [American College of Sports Medicine, *High-intensity interval training*](https://www.acsm.org/docs/default-source/files-for-resource-library/high-intensity-interval-training.pdf). **Visual-model takeaway:** show alternating high-intensity and recovery blocks instead of a continuous effort line.
4. [National Strength and Conditioning Association, *Speed Development for the Tactical Athlete*](https://www.nsca.com/education/articles/kinetic-select/speed-development-for-the-tactical-athlete/). **Visual-model takeaway:** use a compact arm swing and stacked head-ribcage-pelvis at speed.
5. [USA Track & Field, *Coaching Education*](https://www.usatf.org/programs/coaches/coaching-education). **Visual-model takeaway:** show the runner relaxed through the face and shoulders while maintaining a forward-running posture.

**Model baseline:**
- Original SVG requirement 1: a side-view, mid-stride runner with a vertical posture line through ear, shoulder, hip, and stance foot.
- Original SVG requirement 2: a proportional interval strip with exactly two labeled blocks: `60 s hard` and `120 s walk`.
- Original SVG requirement 3: dashed ghost positions may show stride rhythm, but must be newly drawn and have no identifiable person.

## 300-Meter Shuttle Run (300SR)

**H2F cue cross-check:** FM 7-22 identifies the 300-meter shuttle run as anaerobic endurance. The app's 25-meter shuttle, line touch, direction-change, and re-acceleration cues agree with the visual priorities in change-of-direction references. The exact course setup and repetition count remain unit-program decisions.

1. [HQDA, *FM 7-22: Holistic Health and Fitness*, table 6-2](https://armypubs.army.mil/epubs/DR_pubs/DR_a/ARN44522-FM_7-22-002-WEB-7.pdf). **Visual-model takeaway:** draw repeated 25-meter outbound-and-return lanes that sum to the prescribed shuttle distance.
2. [National Strength and Conditioning Association, *Change of Direction Speed*](https://www.nsca.com/education/articles/kinetic-select/change-of-direction-speed/). **Visual-model takeaway:** show a lowered center of mass before the line and a forward body angle on exit.
3. [U.S. Army Training Support Package, *Perform Military Movement Drill 1 (MMD1)*](https://rdl.train.army.mil/catalog-ws/view/100.ATSC/BCCB0FE0-7963-46CA-9882-871078363DC4-1407858196358/report.pdf). **Visual-model takeaway:** use the Army-style shuttle-sprint turn: reach down at the line, then drive out immediately.
4. [National Academy of Sports Medicine, *Agility Ladder Drills*](https://blog.nasm.org/agility-ladder-drills). **Visual-model takeaway:** emphasize short, organized steps into the deceleration zone instead of a single long braking stride.
5. [Human Kinetics, *Teaching the change of direction*](https://us.humankinetics.com/blogs/excerpt/teaching-the-change-of-direction). **Visual-model takeaway:** show the plant foot outside/alongside the turn line and the trunk orienting toward the next travel direction.

**Model baseline:**
- Original SVG requirement 1: a top-down 25-meter lane with two high-contrast boundary lines and directional arrows in both directions.
- Original SVG requirement 2: an enlarged three-pose inset: approach, low line touch, and first acceleration step out.
- Original SVG requirement 3: label distance and turns without showing a finish-time claim, scoring standard, or copied Army diagram.

## Shuttle Sprint

**H2F cue cross-check:** The MMD1 source is the closest Army movement reference. It supports the app's sprint, ground touch, turn, and acceleration cues; it does not justify adding extra cutting technique or changing the programmed number of shuttles.

1. [U.S. Army Training Support Package, *Perform Military Movement Drill 1 (MMD1)*](https://rdl.train.army.mil/catalog-ws/view/100.ATSC/BCCB0FE0-7963-46CA-9882-871078363DC4-1407858196358/report.pdf). **Visual-model takeaway:** model the shuttle sprint as a separate drill movement with a deliberate touch at the turnaround point.
2. [HQDA, *ATP 7-22.02: Holistic Health and Fitness Drills and Exercises*](https://armypubs.army.mil/ProductMaps/PubForm/Details.aspx?PUB_ID=1020967). **Visual-model takeaway:** use a simple sequence of start, sprint, turn, and return consistent with a drill demonstration.
3. [National Strength and Conditioning Association, *Deceleration and Change of Direction*](https://www.nsca.com/education/articles/kinetic-select/deceleration-and-change-of-direction/). **Visual-model takeaway:** show hips dropping and knee flexion before the turn, not after the touch.
4. [USA Football, *Change of Direction and Agility*](https://usafootball.com/blogs/health-safety/change-of-direction-and-agility). **Visual-model takeaway:** point the whole body toward the new direction after the plant rather than twisting only at the knee.
5. [EXOS, *How to improve agility and change of direction*](https://www.teamexos.com/blog/how-to-improve-agility-and-change-of-direction/). **Visual-model takeaway:** make the exit step forceful and low, with the athlete's shin angled in the intended direction of travel.

**Model baseline:**
- Original SVG requirement 1: one uncluttered 25-meter shuttle lane, with a start marker and touch marker.
- Original SVG requirement 2: show a low turn pose with the free hand reaching to the ground and the eyes oriented ahead of the exit.
- Original SVG requirement 3: include one redirection arrow from inbound to outbound travel, keeping arrows outside the athlete silhouette.

## Hill Repeats (up/down)

**H2F cue cross-check:** FM 7-22 calls hill repeats (up and down) anaerobic endurance. The application’s uphill drive and controlled downhill recovery are consistent with hill-running technique sources. Do not depict downhill as another maximal sprint, because its current cue specifies a controlled jog/walk.

1. [HQDA, *FM 7-22: Holistic Health and Fitness*, table 6-2](https://armypubs.army.mil/epubs/DR_pubs/DR_a/ARN44522-FM_7-22-002-WEB-7.pdf). **Visual-model takeaway:** display the up-and-down nature of the repeat rather than an ascent-only hill sprint.
2. [Road Runners Club of America, *Hill Training*](https://www.rrca.org/education-for-runners/hill-training/). **Visual-model takeaway:** show a slight whole-body lean that follows the hill grade, not a bend at the waist.
3. [U.S. Track & Field and Cross Country Coaches Association, *Hill Training*](https://www.ustfccca.org/ustfccca-info/hill-training). **Visual-model takeaway:** use compact arm drive and quick steps on the ascent.
4. [Podium Runner, *How to run hill repeats*](https://www.podiumrunner.com/training/how-to-run-hill-repeats/). **Visual-model takeaway:** make the descent a shorter-stride, controlled-return pose with a lower visual intensity marker.
5. [National Strength and Conditioning Association, *Hill Sprints*](https://www.nsca.com/education/articles/kinetic-select/hill-sprints/). **Visual-model takeaway:** show knee lift and a forceful rear-leg push while keeping the torso aligned with the slope.

**Model baseline:**
- Original SVG requirement 1: a side-view hill with a clearly labeled uphill work arrow and downhill recovery arrow.
- Original SVG requirement 2: show an uphill runner whose torso is one straight line with the incline, plus a separate smaller controlled-descent pose.
- Original SVG requirement 3: use terrain contours and grade ticks created from vector paths only; no terrain photograph or map tracing.

## Burpee (Squat Thrust)

**H2F cue cross-check:** This activity is explicitly marked adapted in the source data, so no Army document is presented as a burpee standard. Its squat-down, feet-back-to-front-leaning-rest, feet-in, and stand/jump sequence agrees with the professional exercise references below. The step-back modification remains an appropriate lower-impact option.

1. [HQDA, *ATP 7-22.02: Holistic Health and Fitness Drills and Exercises*](https://armypubs.army.mil/ProductMaps/PubForm/Details.aspx?PUB_ID=1020967). **Visual-model takeaway:** use front-leaning rest as the Army-recognizable body-position reference for the feet-back phase, without claiming ATP standardizes the burpee.
2. [American Council on Exercise, *Burpee* exercise library](https://www.acefitness.org/resources/everyone/exercise-library/2/burpee/). **Visual-model takeaway:** depict the continuous squat-to-plank-to-return sequence as discrete, readable positions.
3. [National Academy of Sports Medicine, *How to do a burpee*](https://blog.nasm.org/how-to-do-a-burpee). **Visual-model takeaway:** show hands placed before the feet travel back and a braced trunk in the plank position.
4. [Cleveland Clinic, *How to do burpees*](https://health.clevelandclinic.org/burpees). **Visual-model takeaway:** make the landing visibly soft, with hips and knees flexing rather than locked knees.
5. [Verywell Fit, *How to do a burpee*](https://www.verywellfit.com/how-to-do-burpees-3498281). **Visual-model takeaway:** include a step-back/step-in alternate path as a separate low-impact branch, not as an indistinct partial repetition.

**Model baseline:**
- Original SVG requirement 1: four original numbered poses: squat reach, high plank, feet return under hips, vertical finish.
- Original SVG requirement 2: in the plank pose, draw a straight head-to-heel alignment guide and hands under/near shoulders.
- Original SVG requirement 3: a dashed alternative path must label `step back / step in`; it must not imply an Army-tested repetition standard.

## Sprint Intervals (200s/400s)

**H2F cue cross-check:** This source-data entry is adapted programming, not a named Army drill. Its maximal set-distance effort, full recovery, warm-up, and recovery-drill cue is compatible with H2F’s physical-domain framing and sprint-mechanics resources. A visual should preserve the distinction between 200-meter and 400-meter distance options and avoid presenting a universal work-to-rest rule.

1. [U.S. Army H2F, *Physical Domain*](https://h2f.army.mil/Domains/Physical-Domain/). **Visual-model takeaway:** frame the session as deliberate physical-readiness programming rather than a test event diagram.
2. [HQDA, *FM 7-22: Holistic Health and Fitness*, table 6-2](https://armypubs.army.mil/epubs/DR_pubs/DR_a/ARN44522-FM_7-22-002-WEB-7.pdf). **Visual-model takeaway:** pair hard intervals with a clearly separate recovery state, consistent with the anaerobic-endurance category.
3. [USA Track & Field, *Coaching Education*](https://www.usatf.org/programs/coaches/coaching-education). **Visual-model takeaway:** use an upright sprint silhouette with cyclical, opposing arm-leg action for the running portion.
4. [National Strength and Conditioning Association, *Sprint Training for the Tactical Athlete*](https://www.nsca.com/education/articles/kinetic-select/sprint-training-for-the-tactical-athlete/). **Visual-model takeaway:** show acceleration emerging from a slight forward lean and transitioning to tall posture over distance.
5. [Human Kinetics, *Speed training: Sprint mechanics*](https://us.humankinetics.com/blogs/excerpt/speed-training-sprint-mechanics). **Visual-model takeaway:** mark foot strike under the center of mass and a dorsiflexed ankle at ground contact.

**Model baseline:**
- Original SVG requirement 1: a top-down track/lane diagram with interchangeable labels `200 m` and `400 m`, never both portrayed as the same distance.
- Original SVG requirement 2: a side-view sprint sequence that transitions from acceleration lean to upright running using two original poses.
- Original SVG requirement 3: a recovery panel with walk/jog icon and an unquantified `full recovery` label, avoiding a fabricated time prescription.
