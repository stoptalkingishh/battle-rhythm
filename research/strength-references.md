# Muscular-Strength Form References

Selection note: links were checked as publicly reachable reference pages or official publications. The takeaways below describe a visual model, not load prescription or medical advice. ATP 7-22.02 is retained where the local exercise data identifies it as the governing H2F/AFT source.

## Deadlift

**H2F cue cross-check:** "Bar over mid-foot; brace core, flat back"; "Drive through heels; finish by locking hips and knees."

1. [U.S. Army, *ATP 7-22.02: Holistic Health and Fitness Drills and Exercises*](https://armypubs.army.mil/epubs/DR_pubs/DR_a/ARN45013-ATP_7-22.02-001-WEB-4.pdf) - Model the bar starting over the mid-foot and rising close to the legs as hips and knees extend together.
2. [American Council on Exercise, *Deadlift | Exercise Library*](https://www.acefitness.org/resources/everyone/exercise-library/6/deadlift/) - Show a hip-hinged setup with a long, neutral trunk rather than a rounded reach for the bar.
3. [ExRx.net, *Barbell Deadlift*](https://exrx.net/WeightExercises/ErectorSpinae/BBDeadlift) - Depict the shoulders slightly in front of the bar at the floor, followed by a vertical bar path.
4. [Muscle & Strength, *Barbell Deadlift Exercise Guide*](https://www.muscleandstrength.com/exercises/deadlift.html) - Animate the hips driving forward only after the bar has cleared the knees.
5. [CrossFit, *The Deadlift*](https://www.crossfit.com/essentials/the-deadlift) - Use a side view that keeps the lumbar curve fixed while the hips and shoulders rise together.

Model baseline:
1. Original SVG must include a side-view start and finish with the bar centered over the foot.
2. Original SVG must use one upward path arrow that stays close to the shin and thigh.
3. Original SVG must visibly distinguish a neutral spine from an invalid rounded-back posture without copying source artwork.

## Squat

**H2F cue cross-check:** "Brace and descend to parallel"; "Knees track over toes; chest up"; "Drive up through heels."

1. [U.S. Army, *ATP 7-22.02: Holistic Health and Fitness Drills and Exercises*](https://armypubs.army.mil/epubs/DR_pubs/DR_a/ARN45013-ATP_7-22.02-001-WEB-4.pdf) - Model a braced descent to a parallel-thigh depth with the chest held tall.
2. [American Council on Exercise, *Back Squat | Exercise Library*](https://www.acefitness.org/resources/everyone/exercise-library/11/back-squat/) - Show the bar supported across the upper back and the hips traveling down and back under control.
3. [ExRx.net, *Barbell Squat*](https://exrx.net/WeightExercises/Quadriceps/BBSquat) - Use a front/three-quarter view to make knees follow the toe line rather than collapse inward.
4. [Muscle & Strength, *Barbell Squat Exercise Guide*](https://www.muscleandstrength.com/exercises/barbell-squat.html) - Show balanced whole-foot pressure and an ascent led by hip and knee extension.
5. [CrossFit, *The Squat*](https://www.crossfit.com/essentials/the-squat) - Preserve a lumbar curve and keep heels grounded through the bottom position.

Model baseline:
1. Original SVG must show start, parallel-depth, and standing positions.
2. Original SVG must include a toe-to-knee tracking guide in the front or three-quarter view.
3. Original SVG must mark heel contact and a tall-chest/neutral-trunk line.

## Bench Press

**H2F cue cross-check:** "Shoulder blades pinched"; "Bar to mid-chest; elbows ~45 degrees"; "Press and lock."

1. [U.S. Army, *ATP 7-22.02: Holistic Health and Fitness Drills and Exercises*](https://armypubs.army.mil/epubs/DR_pubs/DR_a/ARN45013-ATP_7-22.02-001-WEB-4.pdf) - Model a controlled bar path from the rack to mid-chest and back to a locked-out position.
2. [American Council on Exercise, *Chest Press | Exercise Library*](https://www.acefitness.org/resources/everyone/exercise-library/19/chest-press/) - Show the shoulder blades set back against the bench before the press begins.
3. [ExRx.net, *Barbell Bench Press*](https://exrx.net/WeightExercises/PectoralSternal/BBBenchPress) - Depict forearms nearly vertical at the bottom and a smooth press rather than a chest bounce.
4. [Muscle & Strength, *Barbell Bench Press Exercise Guide*](https://www.muscleandstrength.com/exercises/bench-press.html) - Use a top/three-quarter view to make the approximately 45-degree elbow angle legible.
5. [Stronger by Science, *How to Bench Press*](https://www.strongerbyscience.com/how-to-bench/) - Show a stable upper back and consistent touch point on the mid-chest.

Model baseline:
1. Original SVG must show the bench, rack/bar, and a three-point body contact model: head, upper back, and feet.
2. Original SVG must include a mid-chest touch-point marker and an elbow-angle guide.
3. Original SVG must show retracted scapulae as a simple paired-back-arrow convention, not copied diagrams.

## Pull-Up

**H2F cue cross-check:** "Start from dead hang"; "Pull chin over bar"; "Control the descent."

1. [U.S. Army, *ATP 7-22.02: Holistic Health and Fitness Drills and Exercises*](https://armypubs.army.mil/epubs/DR_pubs/DR_a/ARN45013-ATP_7-22.02-001-WEB-4.pdf) - Model a full hanging start, chin-clearing top, and deliberate return to the hang.
2. [American Council on Exercise, *Pull-ups | Exercise Library*](https://www.acefitness.org/resources/everyone/exercise-library/191/pull-ups/) - Show a long body beneath the bar with no leg kick used to create the upward motion.
3. [ExRx.net, *Pull-up*](https://exrx.net/WeightExercises/LatissimusDorsi/WtPullup) - Depict elbows drawing down toward the ribs while the torso rises toward the bar.
4. [Muscle & Strength, *Pull Up Exercise Guide*](https://www.muscleandstrength.com/exercises/pull-up.html) - Keep the neck neutral as the chin reaches bar height, rather than craning the head forward.
5. [CrossFit, *The Pull-Up*](https://www.crossfit.com/essentials/the-pull-up) - Use separate ascent and descent arrows to reinforce a controlled eccentric.

Model baseline:
1. Original SVG must show a true dead-hang start with elbows extended.
2. Original SVG must include a bar-height line that the chin clears at the top.
3. Original SVG must show a quiet, stacked-leg silhouette and explicitly omit a kipping arc.

## Sumo Squat

**H2F cue cross-check:** "Wide stance; toes slightly out"; "Descend between the feet"; "Knees track over toes."

1. [U.S. Army, *ATP 7-22.02: Holistic Health and Fitness Drills and Exercises*](https://armypubs.army.mil/epubs/DR_pubs/DR_a/ARN45013-ATP_7-22.02-001-WEB-4.pdf) - Model the broad, toe-out lower-body position with the pelvis dropping between the feet.
2. [ExRx.net, *Dumbbell Sumo Squat*](https://exrx.net/WeightExercises/Quadriceps/DBSumoSquat) - Use a front view that shows knees traveling in the same direction as the turned-out toes.
3. [Muscle & Strength, *Sumo Squat Exercise Guide*](https://www.muscleandstrength.com/exercises/sumo-squat.html) - Show planted heels and an upright, braced torso during the descent.
4. [StrengthLog, *Sumo Squat*](https://www.strengthlog.com/sumo-squat/) - Make the central down-and-up hip path clear instead of shifting weight to one side.
5. [ACE Fitness, *Bodyweight Squat | Exercise Library*](https://www.acefitness.org/resources/everyone/exercise-library/135/bodyweight-squat/) - Apply its bodyweight squat depth/control model while retaining the local wide-stance variation.

Model baseline:
1. Original SVG must use a front view with a visibly wider-than-shoulder stance and slightly turned-out feet.
2. Original SVG must include matching knee-to-toe direction lines on both sides.
3. Original SVG must show the pelvis descending centrally between the feet with both heels grounded.

## Straight-Leg Deadlift

**H2F cue cross-check:** "Hinge at hips"; "Bar/load close to legs; flat back"; "Stop at hamstring tension."

1. [U.S. Army, *ATP 7-22.02: Holistic Health and Fitness Drills and Exercises*](https://armypubs.army.mil/epubs/DR_pubs/DR_a/ARN45013-ATP_7-22.02-001-WEB-4.pdf) - Model the hinge as hips moving rearward while the load remains close to the legs.
2. [American Council on Exercise, *Romanian Deadlift | Exercise Library*](https://www.acefitness.org/resources/everyone/exercise-library/317/romanian-deadlift/) - Show a long neutral spine and only a soft knee bend as the hips fold.
3. [ExRx.net, *Barbell Straight-Leg Deadlift*](https://exrx.net/WeightExercises/Hamstrings/BBStraightLegDeadlift) - Stop the downward illustration at the point the hamstrings limit the hinge, not at a forced floor touch.
4. [Muscle & Strength, *Straight Leg Deadlift Exercise Guide*](https://www.muscleandstrength.com/exercises/straight-leg-deadlift.html) - Depict the bar sliding close to the thigh and shin rather than drifting forward.
5. [StrengthLog, *Stiff-Legged Deadlift*](https://www.strengthlog.com/stiff-legged-deadlift/) - Show the return as hip extension with the torso rising as one rigid unit.

Model baseline:
1. Original SVG must contrast hip displacement backward with the nearly vertical load path beside the legs.
2. Original SVG must label a soft-knee, long-spine posture without locking the knees graphically.
3. Original SVG must end the range at a hamstring-tension marker rather than an arbitrary depth.

## Bent-Over Row

**H2F cue cross-check:** "Hinge to ~45 degrees; flat back"; "Pull to lower ribs"; "Squeeze shoulder blades."

1. [U.S. Army, *ATP 7-22.02: Holistic Health and Fitness Drills and Exercises*](https://armypubs.army.mil/epubs/DR_pubs/DR_a/ARN45013-ATP_7-22.02-001-WEB-4.pdf) - Model a fixed hinged torso while the bar travels to the lower-rib area.
2. [ExRx.net, *Barbell Bent-over Row*](https://exrx.net/WeightExercises/BackGeneral/BBBentOverRow) - Show the elbows moving rearward and the trunk staying still rather than being swung upright.
3. [Muscle & Strength, *Bent Over Barbell Row Exercise Guide*](https://www.muscleandstrength.com/exercises/bent-over-barbell-row.html) - Use a side view to preserve approximately 45 degrees of torso inclination and a flat back.
4. [StrengthLog, *Barbell Row*](https://www.strengthlog.com/barbell-row/) - Show a brief shoulder-blade squeeze at the top before the controlled lower.
5. [ACE Fitness, *Single Arm Row | Exercise Library*](https://www.acefitness.org/resources/everyone/exercise-library/126/single-arm-row/) - Apply its elbow-to-rib pulling relationship while retaining the barbell's bilateral hinged stance.

Model baseline:
1. Original SVG must show a 45-degree torso-reference line and a neutral-spine line.
2. Original SVG must show the bar beginning below the shoulders and ending at the lower ribs.
3. Original SVG must use scapular retraction arrows while keeping the hips and torso fixed between frames.

## Overhead Push-Press

**H2F cue cross-check:** "Dip and drive"; "Press overhead; lock out"; "Control descent."

1. [U.S. Army, *ATP 7-22.02: Holistic Health and Fitness Drills and Exercises*](https://armypubs.army.mil/epubs/DR_pubs/DR_a/ARN45013-ATP_7-22.02-001-WEB-4.pdf) - Model a shallow vertical dip followed by a leg drive that sends the bar from the shoulders to overhead lockout.
2. [ExRx.net, *Barbell Push Press*](https://exrx.net/WeightExercises/DeltoidAnterior/BBPushPress) - Show knees and hips extending before the arms finish pressing the bar overhead.
3. [Muscle & Strength, *Barbell Push Press Exercise Guide*](https://www.muscleandstrength.com/exercises/barbell-push-press.html) - Depict the bar starting at the front shoulders and finishing over the mid-foot with straight arms.
4. [StrengthLog, *Push Press*](https://www.strengthlog.com/push-press/) - Keep the torso braced and vertical during the dip-drive instead of turning it into a forward lean.
5. [CrossFit, *The Push Press*](https://www.crossfit.com/essentials/the-push-press) - Separate the dip, drive, press, and controlled return as distinct visual phases.

Model baseline:
1. Original SVG must include four labeled positions: rack, shallow dip, drive/press, and overhead lockout.
2. Original SVG must use a vertical bar-path line over the mid-foot and show locked elbows at the top.
3. Original SVG must include a braced-ribcage/neutral-lumbar cue and reject a hyperextended overhead silhouette.
